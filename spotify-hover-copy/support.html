Spotify Hover Copy — Support

Hover a supported Spotify label and press Ctrl+C or Cmd+C to copy its text.

Contact

Email dmitshostak@gmail.com. Include your Chrome version, operating system, the Spotify view where the problem occurs, and steps to reproduce it. Do not send passwords or account tokens.

Quick checks

  • Use the Spotify web player at open.spotify.com in Chrome.
  • Refresh Spotify after installing or updating the extension.
  • Check that the extension is enabled and allowed to run on Spotify.
  • Hover the title or artist itself before pressing Copy.

Hovered labels take priority even if text is selected in the search field. Away from supported labels, selected text copies normally.

The extension processes labels locally, with no background process, analytics, or network requests. It does not read existing clipboard contents.

This is an independent extension, not affiliated with Spotify. The Spotify desktop app is outside scope.
