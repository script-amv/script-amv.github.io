Spotify Hover Copy — Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: November 11, 2026

Who provides the extension

Spotify Hover Copy is an independent Chrome extension provided by Dmitrii Shostak. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Spotify.

What the extension processes

When you invoke Copy while hovering a supported label on open.spotify.com, the extension reads that label’s visible text and link information locally to identify the song title, artist name, or supported music label. It writes the label text to your system clipboard and briefly changes the label’s appearance to confirm the action. Hover copying takes priority over text selected elsewhere. Away from supported labels, the browser’s normal copy behavior continues.

What the developer collects

The extension does not send data to the developer or to any external server. It has no analytics, advertising, telemetry, account system, or background process. It does not collect or retain browsing history, listening history, cookies, Spotify credentials, search-field contents, or a history of copied labels. It does not read the existing contents of your clipboard. The extension does not make network requests or load remote executable code.

Local processing and retention

The hovered website label is processed on your device only for the copy action and visual feedback. The extension does not save it to extension storage, browser storage, or a developer database. Temporary animation state is discarded when the feedback ends. Text you copy remains in the system clipboard according to your browser and operating system settings until it is replaced or cleared.

Clipboard, pasting, and other services

Your browser and operating system control clipboard access, clipboard history, and any cross-device clipboard synchronization you have enabled. If you paste text into another app or website, that destination handles it under its own policies. Spotify separately handles your use of Spotify under its own policies.

Sharing and Limited Use

The developer does not receive, sell, or transfer data processed by the extension. Website content is used only to provide the extension’s stated copy-and-feedback function. It is not used for advertising, profiling, unrelated purposes, creditworthiness decisions, or lending purposes.

Permissions and your control

The extension runs only on https://open.spotify.com/. It uses the native user-initiated copy event and does not request permission to read clipboard contents. You can disable or remove it in chrome://extensions, or restrict its Spotify site access in Chrome. No developer-held extension data needs to be deleted when you uninstall because the developer does not receive or store it.

This policy page

This privacy page is hosted by GitHub Pages. Loading the page is separate from using the extension; GitHub may process normal website request information under its own privacy statement. This page contains no analytics, advertising, tracking scripts, or external fonts.

Support requests

If you choose to email the developer, your email address, message, and any information you include will be used to respond and investigate the issue. That information is supplied voluntarily, is separate from extension operation, and is retained only as needed to handle the request. You may request deletion by email, subject to any records required by law. Do not send passwords, tokens, or other sensitive account details.

Policy changes

Changes to this policy will be posted here with an updated date. If an extension update changes its data-handling practices, the relevant extension and store disclosures will be updated as required.
